Stanford CS224W: Machine Learning with Graphs, taught by Jure Leskovec. The course covers methods for extracting information from and making predictions about large graphs and networks, including node embeddings, graph neural networks, knowledge graph reasoning, and applications to recommender systems, drug discovery, and social network analysis.
