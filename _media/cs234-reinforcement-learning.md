Stanford CS234: Reinforcement Learning, Spring 2024, taught by Emma Brunskill. The course covers the formal frameworks and techniques for sequential decision making, including Markov decision processes, model-free and model-based reinforcement learning, value function approximation, policy gradient methods, and exploration, together with applications to robotics, healthcare, and education.
