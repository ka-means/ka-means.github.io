A consolidated, Spanish-language study book covering all nine units of an agent-based modeling course taught with NetLogo: foundations of ABM, first models and NetLogo programming, exploration and extension of models, full model construction, architecture, analysis, verification and validation, the history of the field, and advanced modeling. Reconstructed as original pedagogical explanations from the course's transcripts, subtitles, slides and supplementary materials, with added learning objectives, methodological summaries, labs, study questions, exercises, a glossary and references.

Not an official publication of Complexity Explorer, the Santa Fe Institute, Northwestern University, or the course instructors. Original ideas, models and references are attributed within the text and in the final bibliography.
