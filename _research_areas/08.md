**Area 08: Emergent Coordination & Collective Intelligence**

## Master Question

How can global order emerge without anyone designing it?

## What We Want to Discover

Some of the most robust coordination we know of, insect colonies, flocks, markets, was never designed top down. This area studies whether comparable emergent coordination can be engineered deliberately in artificial multi-agent systems, what local rules are sufficient to produce useful global behaviour, and where that emergent order breaks down.

## Why It Matters

Centralized coordination has a single point of failure and a communication bottleneck. If useful coordination can emerge from local rules alone, systems can become more scalable and more resilient at the same time.
