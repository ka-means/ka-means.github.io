**Area 05: Hierarchical RL & Skill Learning**

## Master Question

How can complex behaviour emerge from reusable skills?

## What We Want to Discover

Long horizon tasks are hard to learn one primitive action at a time. This area studies how an agent can discover, represent, and reuse temporally extended skills, and how a higher level controller should choose among those skills to solve tasks that were never encountered as a whole during training.

## Why It Matters

Reusable skills are what let learning transfer across tasks instead of starting from zero every time. Without a principled account of hierarchy, complex behaviour has to be hand engineered task by task, which does not scale.

## Core Concepts

- Options framework
- Subgoal discovery
- Skill composition and skill chaining
- Feudal and manager worker architectures
- Temporal abstraction

## Relevant Disciplines

- Cognitive science, particularly motor skill acquisition
- Linguistics, particularly compositional structure
- Control theory

## Potential Mimicry Sources

- Motor skill hierarchies in animals and humans
- Modularity in biological motor control
- Assembly of complex behaviour from simple reflexes in insects
