**Area 12: Space & Satellite Autonomous Agents**

## Master Question

How should intelligence behave when observation, computation, communication and intervention are scarce?

## What We Want to Discover

A satellite or deep space probe cannot phone home for every decision, has limited onboard compute, and often has only intermittent windows to observe its own state. This area studies how autonomy should be designed under these combined scarcities, and what changes in a decision problem when human intervention is minutes, hours, or simply impossible.

## Why It Matters

Space systems are one of the clearest cases where autonomy is not a convenience but a requirement. Lessons from this area also transfer to any terrestrial system operating with severe communication or compute constraints, such as remote sensors or disaster response robots.

## Core Concepts

- Onboard autonomous decision making
- Resource constrained computation
- Communication windows and latency
- Fault detection, isolation and recovery
- Long duration mission planning

## Relevant Disciplines

- Aerospace engineering
- Astronomy and orbital mechanics
- Systems engineering
- Reliability engineering

## Potential Mimicry Sources

- Deep sea exploration under communication delay
- Wildlife tracking with intermittent connectivity
- Historical exploration under total isolation
