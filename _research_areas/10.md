**Area 10: Distributed Agency**

## Master Question

Where does agency reside when many intelligences control one system?

## What We Want to Discover

A single physical system, such as a vehicle or a spacecraft, can be controlled by a mixture of humans, learned policies, and rule based controllers, none of which has full authority alone. This area studies how agency should be distributed across those controllers, how conflicts between them should be resolved, and where responsibility sits when the combined system acts.

## Why It Matters

Shared control is already common in practice, but it is often implemented ad hoc. A clearer account of distributed agency is needed to reason about accountability, safety, and the actual locus of decision making in these systems.

## Core Concepts

- Shared control and shared autonomy
- Arbitration between controllers
- Responsibility and accountability in composite systems
- Blended authority

## Relevant Disciplines

- Philosophy of action
- Law and liability theory
- Control theory
- Organizational theory

## Potential Mimicry Sources

- Joint action in human teams
- Distributed leadership in animal groups
- Command structures in aviation crews
