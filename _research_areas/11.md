**Area 11: UAV & Mobile Robot Autonomy**

## Master Question

What does it take for an intelligent agent to operate independently in an open world?

## What We Want to Discover

An open world does not come with a fixed map, a fixed set of obstacles, or guaranteed connectivity. This area studies how mobile robots and unmanned aerial vehicles should perceive, localize, plan, and act when the environment is only partially known in advance and can change while the mission is underway.

## Why It Matters

Most practical deployments of ground and aerial robots happen outside controlled environments. Autonomy that only works in a mapped, static warehouse does not transfer to a field, a disaster site, or open airspace.

## Core Concepts

- Simultaneous localization and mapping
- Path planning under uncertainty
- Obstacle avoidance and reactive control
- Mission level autonomy
- Energy aware planning

## Relevant Disciplines

- Robotics engineering
- Aviation
- Geography and cartography
- Control theory

## Potential Mimicry Sources

- Animal navigation without maps
- Insect visual navigation
- Migratory bird route planning
