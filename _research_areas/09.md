**Area 09: Multi-Agent Communication**

## Master Question

What information must intelligences exchange in order to cooperate?

## What We Want to Discover

Communication is costly and bandwidth is finite, so agents cannot simply share everything. This area studies what agents choose to communicate when the content and timing of messages are themselves learned, whether emergent communication protocols are interpretable, and how much communication is actually necessary to achieve a given level of coordination.

## Why It Matters

Communication bandwidth, latency, and reliability are real constraints in any deployed multi-agent or multi-robot system. Knowing the minimum information exchange required for cooperation, rather than assuming unlimited communication, is essential for realistic system design.

## Core Concepts

- Emergent communication protocols
- Message discretization and vocabulary formation
- Communication cost and bandwidth constraints
- Grounded language learning
- Signaling games

## Relevant Disciplines

- Linguistics
- Information theory
- Animal communication studies
- Game theory
