**Project:** [Real Autonomy in Mining — Digital-Twin Experiments](/projects/real-autonomy-in-mining/)

Notebook 3 of the _Real Autonomy in Autonomous Haulage_ series. Compares human-only radio dispatch, human-in-the-loop approval, human-on-the-loop supervision, central autonomy, and a decentralized stigmergic (pheromone-style) control scheme, under a normal shift and under an FMS outage, a shovel failure, and a compound failure of both. Examines approval-latency sensitivity and control-room workload alongside throughput. Site and operator names are anonymized.
