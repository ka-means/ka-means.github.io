**Project:** [Real Autonomy in Mining — Digital-Twin Experiments](/projects/real-autonomy-in-mining/)

Notebook 2 of the _Real Autonomy in Autonomous Haulage_ series. Compares no communication, partial coverage, and full interoperability between the autonomous fleet and heterogeneous external agents (other-manufacturer trucks, manual vehicles, support equipment), isolating how much of the production gain comes from road-level coordination versus a truck–shovel handshake, and how the benefit scales with external-traffic density. Site and operator names are anonymized.
