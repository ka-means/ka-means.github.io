Linear mixed-effects models of craniofacial growth in 27 children followed from ages 8 to 14. Finds a significant sexual dimorphism in growth rate, with boys growing 63.6% faster than girls, and substantial between-subject heterogeneity in both baseline measurements and growth rates.
